## The distance to each missing landmark

- **ER-2 (semantic).** A point estimate of 0.812 against 0.80 is not enough: a lower end of 0.80 needs about 0.86 over
  25 scenarios. Formula alone is at 0.899 [0.854].
- **ER-3.** The semantic miss is under-claiming: test claims are right more often than dev claims at the same
  stability (0.783 against 0.744). Candidates: scenario-level shrinkage of the map, and more scenarios.
- **ER-4a.** Route A cannot come from C3 as built. It takes GES's adjacencies and keeps only directions the PC audit
  licenses, so on F1 it ties GES at best. Route B is reachable in principle. On dev, a perfect tier oracle applied to the
  open pairs alone gains **+0.060 [+0.049, +0.072]** semantic and **+0.044 [+0.036, +0.053]** formula (`analysis/results/q2_b3/ceiling_dev.json`), and
  gpt-4o's recorded tiers capture 43% and 50% of that on dev. Route B needs a lower end above 0.03, so a knowledge source must
  capture about two thirds of the oracle gain on semantic, and nearly all of it on formula.
- **ER-4b.** With 44 answered issues, a lower end of 0.90 needs at most one miss (a point estimate near 0.98). More
  issues would move this; a better ranking would not.

## Findings beyond the gates

1. **Two causal-learn PC defects** (B1): its Meek step can return a directed cycle, and maxP can apply colliders that
   close one. The kernel re-derives PC from its own tests and withdraws cycles. Draft note:
   `docs/drafts/bug_reports/causal_learn_meek_directed_cycle.md` (EXT-07, Uzair decides whether to send).
2. **Names reach the data-only ledger through identical columns** (B3). The name-hash tie-break decides which of
   several identical columns gets which jittered values. D differs across name arms on 0 semantic and 19 formula
   held-out files. Proposal K3-1.
3. **Intervention orientations: collapsed paths, false-positive shifts, weak propagation** (B2). Only 62.5% of formula
   orientations are true edges in that direction, while DV-1 precision is unharmed. On dev, most wrong direct
   orientations are ancestral non-edges (61 of 70 on semantic, 33 of 60 on formula); shift false positives matter on
   formula (27); Meek propagation from them is right only 36-40% of the time. Proposal K3-3.
4. **Data claims fill a badly incomplete topology but hurt a nearly complete one** (B5 gap curve).

## Deviations

- B1: one aborted held-out read (build error), fixed as a class by amendment 1, then a single re-read. Candidate rules'
  stored-row numbers on test were seen before the selection rule was written (disclosed in the B1 pre-registration).
- B4: one replicate per scenario instead of two, changed before any B4 run (recorded in its pre-registration).
- B5: its held-out read ran after B4.1 failed. B5 uses nothing from B4, but the hashed order says a bundle builds only
  on predecessors that passed (recorded in the B5 report).
- B1's full code hash no longer equals its freeze, because B2-B5 code was added after it. The core hash over the
  claim-making files equals B1's in every later freeze (`--same-core-as b1`).

## Where we're going

Building and validating the build needed no live run. What comes next is a choice of which landmark to chase with a
kernel v3, each under its own pre-registration and a fresh held-out set
(`docs/design/q2_kernel_v3_proposals.md`):
- **accuracy:** a direction source beyond the equivalence class for route A, or a stronger knowledge source for route B;
- **calibration:** shrinkage on semantic;
- **RCA:** more issues for ER-4b.
